Changed your mind? Submit again, and your latest pick for the week replaces the last one.

The rules

  • Pick a team you think will lose. Get it right and you survive the week.
  • No repeat picks. Once you have used a team, it is gone for the season.
  • A tie eliminates you. A team that ties did not lose.
  • No teams playing Wednesday, Thursday or Friday. Those games start before the deadline. Saturday games are fine.
  • Teams on a bye cannot be picked.
  • Picks are open Tuesday to Saturday and lock at midnight Central going into Sunday, through Monday. Everyone else's picks stay hidden until they lock.
  • Buy back in: week 1 only.
  • Registration closes when week 1 locks. Nobody joins a pool that is already running.
  • Final four or fewer can split the pot, if everyone still in agrees.

The dropdown lists all 32 teams. Any you cannot pick this week are greyed out with the reason next to them, so you can see why rather than wondering where a team went.
